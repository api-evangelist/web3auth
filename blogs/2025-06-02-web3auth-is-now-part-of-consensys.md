Web3Auth announced its acquisition by Consensys, marking a significant expansion of its wallet infrastructure. The company has grown to nearly 50 million end users powering over 8,200 dApps. The partnership aims to integrate wallet capabilities into MetaMask while maintaining Web3Auth as an independent brand.
